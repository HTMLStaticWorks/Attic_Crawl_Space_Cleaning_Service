Our Restoration Protocol

We follow rigorous hospital-grade decontamination standards to ensure full containment, zero indoor contamination, and long-term protection.

01

Containment & HEPA Extraction

Access portals are sealed with plastic zip walls. Old hazardous insulation and debris are sucked through high-powered negative-air hoses out to exterior trucks.

02

Antimicrobial Fogging & Scrub

All bare framing, subfloors, and joists undergo enzyme disinfectant misting to neutralize mold spores, rodent urine pheromones, and viral colonies.

03

Exclusion Sealing & Re-Insulation

Entry gaps are sealed with steel mesh. Fresh virgin insulation or 20-mil vapor barrier is installed, followed by clearance photo verification.
