Attic Cleaning & Biohazard Decontamination

Over time, attics accumulate toxic rodent droppings, urine pheromones, bat guano, toxic mold spores, and compressed fiberglass dust. Our medical-grade negative-air cleanout extracts all biohazards out to exterior vacuum trucks with zero dust infiltration into living quarters.

Problems Addressed
  • Airborne Pathogen Intrusion: Rodent feces and urine pheromones entering bedrooms through the stack effect.
  • Persistent Odor Trails: Territorial pheromone scents that continuously attract new rodent colonies.
  • Mold & Allergen Reservoirs: High humidity condensation nurturing millions of black mold spores.

Insulation Replacement & Precision Air Sealing

Old, compressed, or rodent-soiled fiberglass loses over 50% of its thermal resistance, driving utility bills sky-high. We vacuum out degraded materials, foam-seal all attic ceiling bypasses, and install fresh, borate-treated R-38 / R-49 insulation.

Problems Addressed
  • High Heating & Cooling Costs: Excessive HVAC runtime caused by degraded thermal barriers.
  • Hidden Chimney Air Leaks: Recessed can lights, plumbing chases, and top plates leaking conditioned air.
  • Hot & Cold Room Inconsistency: Unbalanced indoor climate and drafty rooms.

Crawl Space 20-Mil Vapor Barrier Encapsulation

Unsealed dirt crawl spaces act like a continuous sponge, evaporating groundwater and soil gasses upward into your home. We install heavy-gauge 20-mil multi-layer tear-resistant polyethylene barriers sealed hermetically to foundation walls.

Problems Addressed
  • Groundwater Humidity Evaporation: High relative humidity (>75%) rotting foundation joists.
  • Soil Radon & Musty Odors: Dangerous ground gases and earthy mildew seeping into living rooms.
  • Subfloor Wood Rot Fungi: Timber decay and termite infestations nurtured by damp dirt.

Rodent Proofing & Structural Exclusion

Traps and poison only remove current pests—they do not prevent new rodents from entering. We physically seal every roofline breach, soffit gap, crawlspace vent, and utility pipe chase with chew-proof 1/4" galvanized steel mesh and fire-resistant sealant.

Problems Addressed
  • Recurring Pest Infestations: Constant re-entry of rats, mice, and squirrels through structural gaps.
  • Electrical Fire Hazards: Rodents gnawing through live 120V wiring inside wall cavities.
  • Damaged HVAC Ducting: Pests puncturing flexible air ducts to nest inside heating channels.

Step-by-Step Restoration

How It Works: From Inspection to Completion

Our transparent, clinical 5-step workflow guarantees zero indoor contamination and verified structural protection.

01

Initial Inspection & Audit

Step 1: Diagnostics

Our certified inspector conducts a multi-point thermal scan, joist moisture level test, rodent entry mapping, and digital photo inspection to diagnose all hidden hazards.

02

Containment & Isolation

Step 2: Cleanroom Setup

We establish negative-pressure air isolation with zip-sealed cleanroom doorway portals and heavy-duty floor runners to guarantee zero dust or spore infiltration into living areas.

03

HEPA Extraction & Fogging

Step 3: Decontamination

Exterior vacuum trucks extract contaminated insulation and rodent debris directly outside, followed by ultrasonic hospital virucidal fogging to neutralize mold and pheromones.

04

Structural Sealing & Exclusion

Step 4: Perimeter Defense

All roofline gaps, eave vents, crawlspace access points, and plumbing chaseways are locked down with 1/4" galvanized steel hardware cloth, metal flashings, and expanding polymer barriers.

05

Re-Insulation & Certification

Step 5: Completion & Warranty

Fresh borate-treated R-38 insulation or 20-mil vapor encapsulation is installed. We perform a final air quality check, deliver digital clearance photos, and issue your lifetime warranty.
