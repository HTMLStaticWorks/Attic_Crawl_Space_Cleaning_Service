Insulation Removal

Truck-mounted negative-pressure HEPA vacuums safely remove old, compressed, moisture-damaged, or rodent-fouled fiberglass and cellulose directly to exterior containment tanks without indoor contamination.

Sanitization & Disinfection

Hospital-grade ultrasonic fogging with EPA-registered botanical virucides and enzymatic deodorizers that eradicate black mold spores, rodent urine pheromones, bacteria, and allergen reservoirs.

Rodent-Proofing & Exclusion

Permanent physical exclusion using 1/4" galvanized steel hardware cloth, metal flashings, and pest-resistant expanding polyurethane to seal every crack, soffit vent, and plumbing penetration.

Vapor Barrier Encapsulation

Installation of 20-mil multi-layer tear-resistant polyethylene barriers sealed with waterproof tape to isolate dirt floor humidity, stopping dry rot and subterranean moisture intrusion.

Precision Air Sealing

We identify and seal invisible chimney bypasses—such as recessed lighting canisters, furnace flues, electrical conduits, and wall top plates. Halting the stack effect stops toxic subfloor air from entering living spaces and cuts cooling and heating losses by up to 30%.

HVAC Duct Cleaning & Mastic Sealing

Leaky or contaminated ductwork in unconditioned attics can pull rodent dander, fiberglass glass fibers, and mold spores straight into your HVAC registers. We clean, disinfect, and seal duct joints with fiber-reinforced mastic for pristine airflow.

Moisture Drainage & Sump Systems

Standing water in a crawl space causes foundation settlement and structural wood rot. We install interior perimeter trench drains, automated cast-iron sump pumps with battery backups, and commercial crawl space dehumidifiers.
