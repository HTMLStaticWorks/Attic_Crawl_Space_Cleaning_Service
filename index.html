Critical Home Health Alert

Hidden Hazards Lurking Above & Below

Out of sight is never out of danger. Airborne bio-contaminants travel freely between unfinished spaces and your family's living areas.

Toxic Mold & Fungal Colonies

High crawl space humidity and attic roof condensation nurture toxic black mold, dispersing millions of volatile spores into home HVAC systems.

Rodent Droppings & Pathogens

Rats and mice burrow into warm fiberglass insulation, leaving urine trails and feces that spread Hantavirus, salmonella, and severe respiratory allergens.

The Chimney / Stack Effect

Warm air rising escapes through attic leaks, creating negative suction that pulls damp, contaminated crawl space air directly into your bedrooms.
