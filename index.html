Why Clean Your Attic & Crawl Space?

Unfinished sub-structures directly control up to 40% of the air circulating inside your living rooms and bedrooms.

Eliminate Stack Effect

Warm air rising pulls toxic crawl space humidity, radon, and mold spores upward through floorboards directly into living areas.

Eradicate Bio-Hazards

Rodent feces, urine pheromone trails, and mold colonies emit volatile bio-aerosols that trigger chronic asthma and morning allergies.

Save 30% on Energy

Prevent Structural Rot

Hospital-Grade Protocol

Our Cleaning & Decontamination Process

From toxic contamination to sterile building envelope in five systematic, certified phases.

PHASE 01

Digital Diagnostic Audit

Infrared thermal imaging, joist moisture meters, and airflow sensors identify concealed rodent entry chaseways, moisture intrusion, and air bypass leaks.

PHASE 02

Negative-Air HEPA Vacuuming

High-capacity negative-pressure exterior vacuum trucks extract contaminated insulation through sealed containment ducts directly outside your home.

PHASE 03

Virucidal Ultrasonic Fogging

EPA-registered hospital disinfectants and botanical enzymatic agents are fogged into all timber recesses to eradicate mold spores, bacteria, and rodent urine odors.

PHASE 04

100% Perimeter Exclusion Sealing

Galvanized 1/4" chew-proof steel hardware cloth, metal flashings, and fire-resistant expanding foam permanently seal every plumbing, HVAC, and roofline penetration.

PHASE 05

Thermal Re-Insulation & Certification

Fresh borate-treated virgin R-38 cellulose/fiberglass or multi-layer 20-mil sealed vapor barrier is installed, backed by digital before-and-after clearance certificates.

Critical Home Health Alert

Hidden Hazards Lurking Above & Below

Out of sight is never out of danger. Airborne bio-contaminants travel freely between unfinished spaces and your family's living areas.

Toxic Mold & Fungal Colonies

High crawl space humidity and attic roof condensation nurture toxic black mold, dispersing millions of volatile spores into home HVAC systems.

Rodent Droppings & Pathogens

Rats and mice burrow into warm fiberglass insulation, leaving urine trails and feces that spread Hantavirus, salmonella, and severe respiratory allergens.

The Chimney / Stack Effect

Warm air rising escapes through attic leaks, creating negative suction that pulls damp, contaminated crawl space air directly into your bedrooms.
